## ⚙️ About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **Fırat University (Technology Faculty, 3.36 GPA) 🇹🇷**. I completed my Erasmus+ exchange at **Riga Technical University (RTU) 🇱🇻** in Spring '26, and I'm now back home continuing my studies.
